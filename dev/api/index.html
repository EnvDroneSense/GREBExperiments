GREBExperiments — Module
GREBExperiments

Helpers for experiments that run GREBClimate.jl and reduce its results: annual and area-weighted means, the Gregory sensitivity estimate, map diagnostics. Scripts that use them are in experiments/.

source
GREBExperiments.annual_means — Function
annual_means(records, times, f)

(years, values): for each year with all 12 months among records, the mean over the year of f(record), where f is a field name (the area-weighted mean of that field) or a function of a record that returns a number. times holds the (year, month) of each record.

source
GREBExperiments.summarize — Function
summarize(result)

Reduce a greb_model! result to a few kilobytes: for the control and the scenario, the annual global means of Ts, precip, ice and the top-of-atmosphere net (sw - olr), with their years; whether the scenario is an anomaly; and out_of_range, the first record outside the range (nothing when none; see in_range_report). The control and the scenario are both tested, since a scenario can leave the range while its control is fine.

source
GREBExperiments.in_range_report — Function
in_range_report(records, times, anomaly)

nothing when every record is finite and inside the allowed range, otherwise (year, month, field, value) of the first one that is not. For absolute records the range is that of GREBClimate.RangeCheck() for Ts, Ta, To and q. For anomalies a value is allowed to be as large as the width of that range.

source
GREBExperiments.gregory — Function
gregory(dTs, dnet)

Gregory estimate from annual global means of a surface-temperature change dTs [K] and a top-of-atmosphere net-flux change dnet [W/m2], for example the Ts and net series of the scenario in summarize of an abrupt-CO2 run. Fits dnet = forcing + feedback * dTs.

Returns forcing (W/m2), feedback (W/m2/K), sensitivity (K, the warming at which the fit reaches zero net flux, -forcing / feedback), r2, reached (the last dTs) and remaining (the last dnet). The sensitivity can differ from reached: sw - olr is not the whole budget of the anomaly.

GREBExperiments.area_mean — Function
area_mean(map, mask=nothing)

Area-weighted mean of a (xdim, ydim) map, over the cells where mask is true when a mask is given.

source
GREBExperiments.zonal_mean — Function

The mean over longitude of a map: one value per latitude row.

source
GREBExperiments.polar_amplification — Function
polar_amplification(map; latitude=60)

(north, south): the mean of map poleward of latitude degrees over its global mean, for each hemisphere. A uniform map gives (1.0, 1.0).

source
GREBExperiments.land_ocean — Function
land_ocean(map, z_topo)

(land, ocean): the area-weighted means of map over the land and the ocean cells, land being where the topography z_topo says so.

source
GREBExperiments.pattern_correlation — Function

Area-weighted correlation of two maps, about their own weighted means.

source
GREBExperiments.sine_fit — Function
sine_fit(series, period)

Least-squares fit of c + a sin(2 pi t / period) + b cos(2 pi t / period) to series, t in samples from 0. Returns (amplitude, lag, mean): the amplitude is hypot(a, b) and the lag is in samples, positive when the series peaks later than sin.
