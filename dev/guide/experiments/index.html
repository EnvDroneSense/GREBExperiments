Experiments

Each experiment changes one thing in the model, for example the CO2 level or the energy from the sun. The model first runs a control: a year with no change, at 340 ppm CO2 unless the table gives a different value. It then runs the scenario with the change. The difference between the two is the response of the model climate.

The notebook lists all experiments of GREBClimate. The preset list of GREBClimate gives the exact settings. This page puts them in groups and tells what each group asks.

Groups of experiments

GroupExperimentsQuestionWhat to look at
Constant CO2co2_double (680 ppm), co2_quadruple (1360), co2_10x (3400), co2_half (170), co2_zeroHow much does the climate warm or cool after a sudden change of CO2?The Gregory plot and the effective sensitivity. Compare co2_double and co2_quadruple: is the warming two times larger?
CO2 steps and cyclesco2_abrupt_reverse, co2_sine_waveDoes the climate go back when CO2 goes back? How fast does it follow a CO2 cycle?The time series: the delay between the forcing and the temperature
Sunsolar_plus27, solar_cycle_11yrDoes a change of the sun give the same warming as CO2 at the same forcing?The map: where it warms, compared with co2_double
Emission scenariosrcp26 to rcp85, ssp119 to ssp585, a1b, historical_co2How warm does the model get for each CO2 path? The control is at 280 ppm. The runs start in 1950 (historical_co2 in 1850), The notebook allows 60 scenario years, so it does not reach the 21st century.The time series of the global mean
Regional CO2regional_co2_nh, _sh, _tropics, _extratropics, _ocean, _land_ice, _winter, _summerDoes the warming stay where the CO2 increases?The map: the warming inside and outside the region
Ocean surfacesst_plus1, elnino, lanina, rcp85_boundaryHow does the atmosphere respond to a given change of the sea surface?The map
Earth orbit and paleoclimateobliquity, eccentricity, earth_sun_distance, paleo_231kyr and two variantsHow does the orbit of the Earth change the climate?The map and the seasons. These runs give absolute values, not changes, so the notebook shows no Gregory plot.

Before you compare two runs

  • Use the same control years and scenario years for both runs.
  • A run must stay in range: out_of_range must be (ctrl = nothing, scnr = nothing).
  • A short run shows the fast response only. The ocean warms slowly, so the warming continues after 30 years.
  • GREB is a simple model with fixed clouds and a fixed atmospheric circulation. Its result is an estimate. It can differ from the result of a full climate model.

Order to try

  1. co2_double: the reference experiment. Read the Gregory plot.
  2. co2_quadruple and co2_half: is the response linear in the CO2 level, or in its logarithm?
  3. solar_plus27: compare its map with that of co2_double.
  4. A regional experiment: does the warming stay in the region?
