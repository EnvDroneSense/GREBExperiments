Sensitivity estimate

gregory estimates how strongly the model responds to a forcing. It uses one abrupt-forcing run and does not wait for equilibrium. The Guide page Climate sensitivity explains the method.

The fit

gregory(dTs, dnet) fits the line $N = F + \lambda \, \Delta T$ to the annual global means. The forcing $F$ is the fit at zero warming, the feedback $\lambda$ is the slope, and the sensitivity $-F / \lambda$ is the warming at which the fit reaches zero net flux. The docstring of gregory lists all fields of the result.

julia> using GREBExperiments

julia> dT = [0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0];

julia> g = gregory(dT, 5.0 .- 2.0 .* dT);

julia> (g.forcing, g.feedback, g.sensitivity)
(5.0, -2.0, 2.5)

julia> (g.reached, g.remaining)
(2.0, 1.0)

With a model run

Use the Ts and net series of the scenario in the summarize result of an abrupt-CO2 preset, such as :co2_double:

using GREBClimate, GREBExperiments

fields = load_climatology(greb_data_dir(; allow_download=false); dataset=:ncep)
result = greb_model!(RunSpec(ctrl=5, scnr=100), preset(:co2_double; corrections=SpinUp(3));
    jld2_dir=greb_data_dir(; allow_download=false), fields=fields)
s = summarize(result)
g = gregory(s.scnr.Ts, s.scnr.net)

scripts/sensitivity.jl does this and prints the result. See Running the scripts.

Limits of the estimate

  • The sensitivity is an effective sensitivity. It comes from the first decades of a run, not from equilibrium. The deep ocean has not warmed yet, so a longer run can give a different value.
  • It can differ from reached, because sw - olr is not the full energy budget of the anomaly. The docstring of gregory gives the two other flows. closure shows the ratio, and remaining shows how much of the imbalance stays at the end.
  • It has a meaning only for a preset that gives its scenario as an anomaly (scnr_anomaly is true) and applies its forcing at one time.
  • A low r2 shows that the response is not a straight line over the years used, for example because of sea ice or a forcing that changes. Do not use the sensitivity then.
